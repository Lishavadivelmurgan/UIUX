Published in 1925, The Great Gatsby by F. Scott Fitzgerald is a classic American novel set in the summer of 1922 during the Roaring Twenties.As noted by Britannica, “Set in Jazz Age New York, it tells the story of Jay Gatsby, a self-made millionaire, and his pursuit of Daisy Buchanan, a wealthy young woman whom he loved in his youth.”The story is told through the eyes of Nick Carraway, a young man who moves to Long Island next door to Gatsby’s extravagant mansion and becomes entangled in the lives of his affluent cousin Daisy and her wealthy husband, Tom Buchanan. According to SparkNotes, the novel “explores themes of wealth and class, with Gatsby's pursuit of success and love serving as a symbol of the elusive and often unattainable nature of the American Dream.” Ultimately, the glittering parties and social facade give way to tragic consequences and moral disillusionment.
